Bespoke Architectural Finishes

Explore our eight primary luxury flooring sectors. We provide meticulous installations matching your home's unique footprint.

01 / TIMBER SECTOR

Solid Hardwood & Chevron Lay

Our hardwood selections are the ultimate choice for estate developers. We design classic Wide Planks, Herringbone, Chevron patterns, and vintage French Marquetry. Select from premium European White Oak, American Black Walnut, and hard exotics, calibrated to your heating systems.

  • Dynamic moisture acclimation protocols prior to installation.
  • Micro-beveling for flat seamless structural board limits.
  • Multi-layer executive UV oils or extreme matte finishes.

Luxury Carpeting & Custom runners

Experience absolute sensory luxury. We source magnificent deep wool-piles, natural linen-weaves, hand-woven sisal textures, and silk accent broadloom carpets. Ideal for quiet personal chambers, grand stairs, and residential library structures.

  • Pure wool and natural fibers, safe and hypoallergenic.
  • Custom stairs double-stretch padding configurations.
  • Hand-sewed surging edges for visual prestige.

03 / RESILIENT SECTOR

Bespoke Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT)

LVT reproduces wood grains and marble surfaces with exceptional waterproofing and wear resistance. Ideal for luxury wellness bathrooms, personal indoor athletic courts, high-traffic corridors, or mudrooms.

  • 100% waterproof layers, perfect for high-humidity areas.
  • Hardened wear barrier limits, resistant to scratches.
  • Dynamic acoustic cushioning core blocks.

04 / STRUCTURED SECTOR

High-End Laminate layouts

Our specialized high-density fiber board (HDF) master laminates use photographic imagery to replicate wood patterns. We specify only high AC ratings suitable for active households with pets or busy spaces.

  • Extremely scratch, fade, and pressure indentation proof.
  • Click-lock seal edges to protect against spills.
