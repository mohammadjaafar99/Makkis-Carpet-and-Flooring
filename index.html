Artisinal Protocol

Your Journey To Perfection

Continuous refinement from initial hand-drawn proposals to micrometrical tolerances upon final sweep.

01

Architectural Consultation

We deliberate on tone dynamics, architectural styles, thermal underlayment needs, and acoustics. Our designers establish your tailored master schedule.

02

Micrometrical Evaluation

Our surveyors travel to your estate for high-precision digital scanning and structural evaluation of the sub-floor surface thickness and damp layouts.

03

Curation & Sourcing

Your flooring timber of selective grains or custom dyed looms is prepared. All materials are certified through strict quality inspection blocks before shipping.

04

Precision Integration

Experienced master technicians install every board or loom, aligning angles flawlessly. Edge bounds are crafted by hand to prevent raw cuts from exposure.

05

Aesthetic Inspection

We conduct a micro-alignment sweep, checking wood expansion gaps and carpet tension. The workspace is handed over in immaculate condition for occupancy.
