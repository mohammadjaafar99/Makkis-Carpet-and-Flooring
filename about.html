SOCIETAL PILLARS of Craft

A Relentless Obsession with Flawless Surface Finishes.

What began as single-fittings for heritage wooden library vaults has expanded into an internationally sourced design enterprise. Makki’s Carpet & Flooring integrates standard installations with highly developed custom architecture. We source European White Oak logs directly from sustainable managed state forests, milling custom planks in dimensions tailored to your hallway ratios.

In carpeting, our direct associations with premium loom weavers allow us to customize pile height compositions, producing carpets of magnificent tactile presence. Every stair runner is cut, surged, and tucked using hand-aligned luxury linen thread to avoid structural buckling over time.

Acoustic Architecture

Extreme Climate Tolerances

Meticulous subfloor relative-humidity calibrations to guarantee stability through the seasons.

Artisanal Standards

The Makki Promise

Four strict protocols we perform on every residence to maintain absolute fidelity.

Calibration

Digital moisture probe scanning on seven areas of concrete pads to ensure zero under-timber dampness leaks.

Seamless Joints

Sub-millimeter plank alignments using structural tension straps, preventing wood clicking, gaps or expansion bumps.

Ultra-Pure Underlayment

Specifying premium cork and high-density memory latex padding to give carpets a cloud-like sensation.

Direct Sourcing

We maintain exclusive supply networks directly with certified mills in Europe and premier US lumber producers.
